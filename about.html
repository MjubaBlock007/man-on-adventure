About Man on Adventure Safaris

Your Gateway to Extraordinary African Adventures

Our Story

Born from a deep love for Tanzania's wilderness, Man on Adventure Safaris began as a dream to share Africa's magic with the world.

Founded by local guides with over 15 years of experience, we've grown from a small team of passionate naturalists to one of Tanzania's most trusted safari operators. Our journey began in the heart of Arusha, where we realized that true safari experiences come not just from seeing wildlife, but from understanding the land, its people, and its delicate ecosystems.

Today, we pride ourselves on creating personalized adventures that connect travelers with the authentic spirit of Africa. From the vast plains of the Serengeti to the pristine beaches of Zanzibar, every journey with us is crafted with care, expertise, and a genuine love for our homeland.
