Climb Mount Kilimanjaro

Climbing Kilimanjaro is a demanding but deeply rewarding journey to the roof of Africa. The trail winds through five distinct climate zones — rainforest, moorland, alpine desert, and finally arctic summit — before reaching Uhuru Peak at 5,895 meters. Choose from six proven routes below based on your fitness level, schedule, and preferred pace of acclimatization.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to climb Kilimanjaro?

Between 5 and 9 days depending on the route. Longer routes give better acclimatization and higher summit success rates.

Is Kilimanjaro a technical climb?

No — no ropes or climbing gear are needed, but the trek is physically demanding due to altitude and terrain.

What is the best time of year to climb?

January to mid-March and June to October offer the driest, clearest conditions.
