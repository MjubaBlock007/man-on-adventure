Overview

Explore Africa's greatest wildlife parks and tropical islands on this 15-day ultimate Tanzania & Zanzibar journey.

Start in Arusha before embarking on an unhurried exploration of the northern safari circuit — featuring iconic national parks, legendary big cats, and two of Africa's Seven Natural Wonders: the vast Serengeti plains and the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater.

Driven in your private 4x4 Toyota Land Cruiser by your expert safari guide, this private itinerary offers complete flexibility to travel at your own pace. After your thrilling wildlife adventure, board a domestic flight directly to the spice island of Zanzibar for a full week of relaxation along turquoise waters and white sand beaches. Every accommodation tier on this trip is hand-picked by our team of Man On Adventure travel experts.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to visit Tanzania and Zanzibar?

Tanzania offers incredible wildlife viewing year-round. The dry season from June to October is ideal for Great Migration sightings and clear beach weather in Zanzibar.

What can I expect from a safari with Man On Adventure Safaris?

Expect dedicated 24/7 service, private customized 4x4 Land Cruisers with pop-up roofs, knowledgeable local guides, carefully selected lodges, and zero-stress logistical planning.

How can I personalize my 15-day trip with your services?

All itineraries are 100% customizable. You can adjust accommodation tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum), extend beach days, or add activities like Serengeti hot air balloon safaris and scuba diving.

Can I see the Big Five on this package?

Yes! The combination of Tarangire, Serengeti, and the Ngorongoro Crater floor provides optimal opportunities to spot lions, leopards, elephants, rhinos, and buffaloes.

Is it safe to travel to Tanzania and Zanzibar?

Absolutely. Tanzania is one of Africa's most peaceful and welcoming destinations. Your private guide will ensure safety, comfort, and seamless transfers throughout your journey.

Day-by-Day Details

Safari Phase · Days 1–8
Day 1

Arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) – Arusha

As you walk out of Kilimanjaro International Airport, your personal Man On Adventure guide greets you with a warm smile: "Karibu Tanzania – welcome to Tanzania!" Your guide takes you straight to your hotel in Arusha, where you can relax and take in the African atmosphere. The next day your safari adventure begins in your exclusive private jeep.

Silver: Ahadi Lodge / Planet Lodge

Gold: Arusha Villa / Mount Meru Hotel

Platinum: Giraffe Manor Tanzania / Gran Meliá Arusha

Day 2

Tarangire National Park

Depart after breakfast for Tarangire National Park, famous for its giant baobab trees and massive elephant herds. Explore the Tarangire River basin, where lions, leopards, giraffes, and zebras congregate. Enjoy a picnic lunch in the wild before an afternoon game drive.

Silver: Sangaiwe Tented Lodge

Gold: Tarangire Safari Lodge

Platinum: Tarangire Treetops by Elewana

Day 3

Lake Manyara National Park

Lake Manyara National Park lies between Tarangire National Park and the Ngorongoro Crater, right along the 400-meter-deep Great Rift Valley escarpment. The park boasts varied landscapes that attract many species, including dense groundwater forests filled with baboons, birdlife, elephants, and tree-climbing lions.

Silver: Manyara Best View Lodge and Spa

Gold: Manyara's Secret

Platinum: Lake Manyara Kilimamoja Lodge by Wellworth

Day 4

Lake Manyara to Central Serengeti National Park

Journey through the lush Ngorongoro Highlands before descending into the endless plains of the Serengeti. The Central Serengeti (Seronera) offers year-round wildlife action, big cat sightings, and granite rock formations (kopjes) where lions rest.

Silver: Serengeti Sound of Silence Tented Camp

Gold: Signature Serengeti Luxury Tented Camp

Platinum: Serengeti Sametu Camp by Karibu Camps and Lodges

Day 5–6

Serengeti National Park (Full Day Exploration)

Serengeti National Park is widely known as the premier safari destination in Africa, mainly because of the Great Migration that passes through here. The vast stampeding herds are impressive, as are the predators that live here: over four thousand lions, cheetahs, leopards, and hyenas. Spend two full days tracking game with your private guide. (Optional: Serengeti Hot Air Balloon ride with champagne breakfast.)

Silver: Serengeti Sound of Silence Tented Camp

Gold: Signature Serengeti Luxury Tented Camp

Platinum: Serengeti Sametu Camp by Karibu Camps and Lodges

Day 7

Serengeti to Ngorongoro Conservation Area

Enjoy a final morning game drive through the Serengeti as you travel toward the rim of the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Arrive at your crater-rim lodge in time for an unforgettable African sunset over the caldera.

Silver: Rhino Lodge / Manyara Best View Lodge

Gold: Ngorongoro Serena Safari Lodge

Platinum: Ngorongoro Oldeani Mountain Lodge by Wellworth / Crater Lodge

Day 8

Ngorongoro Crater Tour – Flight to Zanzibar

The famous Ngorongoro Crater is one of Africa's Seven Natural Wonders, formed two million years ago when a vast volcano erupted. The fertile crater floor hosts the highest concentration of wildlife in Africa, including black rhinos, lions, and hippos. After a morning crater safari, transfer to Arusha/Kilimanjaro for your flight to Zanzibar.

Silver: Tembo House Hotel (Stone Town)

Gold: Dhow Palace Hotel

Platinum: Park Hyatt Zanzibar

Zanzibar Phase · Days 9–15
Day 9

Historic Stone Town & Spice Plantation Tour

Immerse yourself in Zanzibar's history with a guided walking tour of Stone Town's narrow alleys, historic doorways, and bustling night markets. Later, visit a local spice farm to touch, smell, and taste fresh cloves, vanilla, nutmeg, and cinnamon.

Silver: Amaan Beach Bungalows (Nungwi)

Gold: Ocean Paradise Resort & Spa

Platinum: Royal Zanzibar Beach Resort / Zuri Zanzibar

Day 10

Zanzibar Beach Relaxation (Nungwi / Kendwa)

Relax along white sand beaches beside clear turquoise waters. Enjoy a day at leisure swimming, reading under palm trees, or sipping fresh cocktail drinks on the shore.

Silver: Amaan Beach Bungalows

Gold: Ocean Paradise Resort & Spa

Platinum: Royal Zanzibar Beach Resort / Zuri Zanzibar

Day 11

Mnemba Atoll Marine Safari & Snorkeling

Board a traditional wooden dhow boat for a sea excursion to Mnemba Atoll Conservation Area. Snorkel over coral reefs filled with tropical fish and sea turtles, accompanied by a fresh seafood barbecue lunch on the beach.

Silver: Amaan Beach Bungalows

Gold: Ocean Paradise Resort & Spa

Platinum: Royal Zanzibar Beach Resort / Zuri Zanzibar

Day 12

Leisure Beach Day & Water Sports

Enjoy a full free day in tropical paradise. Choose to relax by the resort pool, take a paddleboard out onto the calm bay, or book an oceanfront spa massage.

Silver: Amaan Beach Bungalows

Gold: Ocean Paradise Resort & Spa

Platinum: Royal Zanzibar Beach Resort / Zuri Zanzibar

Day 13

Jozani Red Colobus Forest & Sunset Dhow Cruise

Take a guided nature walk through Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park, the last sanctuary of the rare Zanzibar Red Colobus Monkey. In the late afternoon, set sail on a romantic sunset dhow cruise along the northern coast.

Silver: Amaan Beach Bungalows

Gold: Ocean Paradise Resort & Spa

Platinum: Royal Zanzibar Beach Resort / Zuri Zanzibar

Day 14

Final Paradise Day in Zanzibar

Spend your last full day unwinding on the beach, buying local artisan crafts, and enjoying a farewell beachfront dinner under the stars.

Silver: Amaan Beach Bungalows

Gold: Ocean Paradise Resort & Spa

Platinum: Royal Zanzibar Beach Resort / Zuri Zanzibar

Day 15

Outbound International Flight Home from Zanzibar

Enjoy a final breakfast with tropical views before your private transfer to Zanzibar International Airport (ZNZ) for your flight home. Leaving with unforgettable memories of your 15-day safari and island paradise adventure!
